It combines survivor-style combat, multi-character squads, Sigil Board formations, giant boss battles, danger levels, and a tower-defense Base Defense mode.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Load into a playable stage before activating movement options. Use the speed multiplier to reposition quickly around enemy waves, collect stage drops, protect the base, or create distance from giant bosses. Return the value to normal before leaving the stage.

### Tools & Inventory

The game's build management is centered on selecting characters, forming a squad, and arranging Sigils on the Sigil Board rather than managing a traditional loot-heavy inventory. Configure the desired squad and Sigil formation in the game's own menus, then use the trainer to support the resulting combat setup.

### World & Environment

Stages are built around surviving enemy waves and completing combat objectives. Base Defense stages add a central structure, defensive towers, obstacles, and route control. Use protection and movement options carefully when switching between standard survivor stages and defense-focused challenges.

### Combat & Weapons

Combat combines direct character control with automatic or recurring survivor-style attacks, squad abilities, Sigil Board formations, and boss encounters. Activate health protection before entering dense enemy waves, then use damage or skill options when testing builds, clearing high danger levels, or facing giant bosses.
